The Problem

DeFi lending liquidations cost users billions every year.

Existing protection tools introduce the very risks DeFi was built to eliminate: centralization, uptime dependency, and persistent infrastructure costs.

🤖

Off-chain bots

Require persistent uptime, private key management, and custom infrastructure — inaccessible to most users.

🔗

Chainlink Keepers

Centralized executor layer with recurring gas costs and a trusted third party in the critical path.

😴

Manual monitoring

Users liquidated while sleeping or offline — volatility strikes at the worst possible moment.

🔒

Single-chain only

No existing solution lets you protect a position on Chain A using reserves held on Chain B.

The Solution

ReactGuard: Always-on protection, fully on-chain.

A Reactive Contract that watches your lending position and automatically deposits collateral from your reserve wallet on another chain — the moment your health factor drops.

How It Works

4 steps. Zero off-chain components.

01

Monitor

A Reactive Contract on Reactive Network subscribes to health factor events emitted by your lending protocol (e.g. Aave on Polygon). It watches 24/7 inside ReactVM.

02

Detect

When your health factor drops below your configured threshold (e.g. 1.2), the RC fires immediately — no delay, no missed blocks.

03

Instruct

The RC dispatches an authenticated cross-chain callback to your Reserve Vault on Chain B (e.g. Arbitrum), specifying the required top-up amount.

04

Protect

The Vault releases your reserve collateral, bridges it to Chain A, and deposits it into your lending position — liquidation prevented, all on-chain.

⚡

Reactive Smart Contract

Event-driven logic running inside ReactVM. Subscribes, evaluates, and acts in one atomic flow.

🏦

Reserve Vault

User-scoped smart contract on the destination chain. Holds reserves, validates callbacks, releases assets.

🌉

Cross-Chain Bridge

Native bridge integration to move assets from Chain B to Chain A safely and verifiably.

🖥️

Config UI

Simple interface to set your health factor threshold, reserve chain, asset, and max top-up amount.
